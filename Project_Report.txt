SALES & DEMAND FORECASTING FOR BUSINESSES

1. OBJECTIVE
Build a machine-learning model to forecast future monthly sales using historical business data.

2. DATA
A realistic sample retail dataset was generated for demonstration because no business dataset was supplied.
It contains daily sales from January 2023 through December 2025 for Electronics, Grocery and Fashion.
The daily data was aggregated to monthly sales for forecasting.

3. DATA PREPROCESSING
- Converted dates to datetime format.
- Sorted observations chronologically.
- Aggregated daily sales to monthly sales.
- Checked the chronological structure before modelling.

4. FEATURE ENGINEERING
- Year
- Month
- Quarter
- Cyclical month features (sine/cosine)
- 1-month, 2-month and 3-month sales lags
- 3-month rolling average

5. MODEL
Random Forest Regression was used because it can model nonlinear relationships between time-based and lag-based features.

6. MODEL EVALUATION
The final six historical months were kept as a chronological test set.
Metrics used:
- MAE: Mean Absolute Error
- RMSE: Root Mean Squared Error
- MAPE: Mean Absolute Percentage Error

Average MAPE across the three product categories: 7.67%.

The category with the lowest validation MAPE in this generated example was Grocery.
This is a descriptive result for the sample data, not a guarantee for real business data.

7. FORECAST
The model was retrained using the full historical period and used recursively to forecast the next six months.

8. BUSINESS USE
Forecasts can support:
- Inventory planning
- Procurement decisions
- Staffing
- Promotion planning
- Revenue planning
- Identifying seasonal demand patterns

9. VISUALIZATION
The project includes historical sales trends, six-month forecasts, forecast-error comparison and forecast summary charts.
The CSV and Excel outputs are also structured for Power BI import.

10. LIMITATION
The sample dataset is simulated. For a real submission, replace it with actual company/Kaggle/public historical sales data and rerun the model.
